# He Mailed Himself Home

Format: YouTube Short (cap 60 s). Slug: `mailed-himself-home`.
Alternative titles: "The World's Cheapest Flight" · "He Flew Home Cash on Delivery"

## Script (beats labeled)

**[1 HOOK]**
In 1964, a man flew from London to Australia with no ticket. He mailed himself.

**[2 DROP-IN]**
Reg Spiers, a javelin thrower, is stuck in London. Thieves took his wallet and all his savings. And his daughter's birthday is coming.

**[3 ESCALATION]**
So his friend John builds a wooden crate, just big enough for one athlete.
Reg climbs in with food, water, a torch… and an empty bottle.
The label: cash on delivery.
Fog keeps the plane grounded for hours.
In Bombay, workers leave the box upside down on a scorching runway for four hours.
Reg strips off his clothes and waits.

**[4 DOUBT]**
Back in London, John hears nothing and fears the worst.

**[5 "YOU SEE,"]**
You see, Reg had worked in airport cargo. Ship something cash on delivery, and the receiver pays.

**[6 PAYOFF + TWIST]**
Sixty-three hours later, in Perth, he cuts his way out with a pocketknife and hitchhikes home to Adelaide.
He makes the birthday. And the bill for the world's cheapest flight? He never paid it.

## Fact notes (how the script handles conflicting sources)

- **London fog delay:** sources give 20, 24 or 28 hours, so the script says "for hours".
- **Wallet:** most accounts, including Athletics Weekly, say it was stolen. One calls it "lost". The script says thieves took it.
- **Injury and Olympic team:** sources conflict on which team he tried for, so the script leaves both out.
- **Bombay:** upside down on the tarmac for about four hours in the heat, and he stripped off. Sources agree.
- **Packing list:** food, water, a torch and a container for urine are the common core. The script names only these.
- **Escape:** he cut his way out with a pocketknife, then hitchhiked to Adelaide. Accounts differ on how he left the airport, so the script skips that.
- **Freight bill:** a 1964 Reuters report said Air India expected him to pay. Later accounts say he never did and it wasn't pursued.
